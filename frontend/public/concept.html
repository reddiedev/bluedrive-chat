Bard Identity sheet · v1

The thesis

Every word you say here, stays in this room.

Bard is a local-first chat app. The interface is a transcript of a private recitation — a warm spoken voice in a cool machine, with the stream itself made visible.

Signature — the recitation trace

One memorable element: a thin brass field that makes the stream visible. In the composer it rests flat when idle, rises and falls as tokens actually arrive, and settles when the response ends. In the sidebar each saved thread keeps a tiny fingerprint derived from that conversation's real rhythm — so threads are recognised by their shape rather than an emoji. Same component, two scales.

Notes

Colour is spent once: brass is the voice, reserved for the trace, focus, and the primary action. Everything around it is quiet. The dark ground is a coloured indigo ink rather than black; the light ground is a cool mineral porcelain rather than cream. Gradients are absent. The only hard hairline in the system is the door rule on the landing.
